# ExamCoach — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Vision
Become Indonesia's leading AI-powered personalized exam preparation ecosystem.

## Mission
Help users study smarter through personalization, measurable insights, adaptive practice, and sustainable habits.

## Initial Market
CPNS preparation, initially focusing on TIU. Architecture must support future BUMN, TPA, UTBK/SNBT, and English tests.

## Problems
- Excessive ads interrupt learning.
- Aggressive paywalls reduce trust.
- Generic question banks do not personalize learning.
- Users cannot identify precise weaknesses.
- Scores are shown without actionable next steps.
- Users struggle with consistent study habits.

## Differentiation
ExamCoach is a learning intelligence platform combining deterministic weakness analysis, recommendations, adaptive drilling, readiness insights, lightweight AI Coach, and habit building.

## Branding
Serious & Professional: trustworthy, calm, focused, intelligent, credible. Gamification remains minimal and clean.

## AI Positioning
AI Coach explains structured learning insights, summarizes performance, generates study schedules, and motivates users. AI must not determine scoring, correctness, weakness calculations, or adaptive selection.

## Monetization
Freemium + subscription.

### Free
- 1 tryout/day
- result visible
- limited explanations
- basic weakness insight
- no ads during active tests

### Premium 1
Target promotional price: Rp39k/month.
- increased test access
- AI insight about 1x/day
- personalized study schedule
- expanded insights

### Premium 2
Target promotional price: up to Rp99k/month.
- higher AI quota
- unlimited adaptive drilling
- more frequent AI guidance
- targeted weakness-focused practice

Pricing and quotas must be configurable.

## Go-To-Market
Launch approximately 2–3 months before the target official examination period.

## Content
Use original content, legally licensed material, and generated questions based on validated patterns. Copyright/licensing compliance is mandatory.

## KPIs
- D1/D7 retention
- weekly active learners
- tryout completion
- recommendation engagement
- adaptive drill completion
- score improvement
- subscription conversion
- AI cost per active learner
- percentile/readiness improvement

## Long-Term
Phase 1: CPNS consumer app.
Phase 2: multi-exam platform.
Phase 3: broader learning intelligence ecosystem.
Phase 4: B2B school/bimbel and teacher analytics, targeted around two years after consumer validation.

## Principle
> Optimize measurable learning improvement, not the number of questions answered.
